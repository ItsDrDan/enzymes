# Enzyme Engine (CHEM 3B Chapter 11: Enzymes and Vitamins)

Study game for CHEM 3B Chapter 11, matched to the Lecture 23–24 enzyme slides. Live at https://itsdrdan.github.io/enzymes/

- `index.html`: the whole game (standalone; no build step). Loads the brand kit (`games/brand/itsdrdan.css`, `icons.js`, `embed.js`), `games/track.js` for anonymous stats, and the Exam Practice module from the textbook repo (`textbook/js/fbx.js`, questions from `textbook/bank/enzymes.json`).
- 6 main options → 20 topics: Enzyme Basics (key terms, label the enzyme, lock & key vs induced fit, activation energy, specificity), Classify Enzymes (six classes, name clues, from the structures: a reactant and product structure from the metabolism pathways, name the enzyme class), Enzyme Activity (read the graph, predict the rate), Inhibitors & Control (spot the inhibitor, regulation), Cofactors & Vitamins (cofactors, B vitamins → coenzymes, deficiencies, water vs fat soluble, classify vitamins), Health & Clinic (dental hygiene chairside, meds & nursing care, lab tests, enzyme & vitamin disorders). Plus Skill Check Prep (10 questions → study plan) and Exam Practice.
- `ASSETS.md`: the art list (mascot Enzo, logo, badges). Stand-ins show until the files exist.
- Links: `?topic=six,names` opens topics directly; the old `?mode=cls|inh|plot|vit|reg|exam` links still work.

Inhibitors: competitive (looks like substrate, active site, overcome by more substrate) · noncompetitive (other site, changes shape, not overcome) · irreversible (covalent: aspirin, penicillin, nerve agents) · heavy metals (bind –SH; irreversible and noncompetitive) · feedback (end product, allosteric). Activity rises with temperature until denaturation (~40 °C for human enzymes), peaks at an optimum pH (pepsin 2, trypsin 8, most others ~7), and plateaus when substrate saturates the active sites.

B vitamins → coenzymes: B1 TPP · B2 FAD · B3 NAD+ · B5 CoA · B6 PLP · B7 biotin · B9 THF · B12 cobalamin. Vitamin C: collagen (scurvy). Vitamin K: clotting. Fat-soluble A, D, E, K are stored; water-soluble B and C are not.

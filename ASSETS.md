# Enzyme Engine: art

**Status (Nov 4, 2026):** all 25 images are in (ChatGPT set, checked: transparent cut-outs, one stray speck removed from enzo-oops). The study hub card image `games/assets/games/enzymes.webp` goes in the games repo.

## Original request

**29 images total: 1 logo + 7 menu badges + 12 achievement badges + 5 mascot moods (+ 1 study hub card copy of the logo).**

Use the brand prompt from itsdrdan.github.io/games/brand and change only the Subject line. Glossy cartoon style, transparent background, no text except the letters/symbols listed.

Diagrams inside questions (the enzyme with its active site, inhibitor pictures, energy diagram, activity graphs, the A→B→C→D pathway) are drawn by the game's code on purpose: students tap parts of them, and the curves and labels must be exact. Don't make those.

Until an image exists, the game shows a stand-in (brand-kit badge, or a drawn Enzo). Drop a file in with the exact name below and the game uses it automatically. No code changes needed.

## The mascot: Enzo the enzyme (5 moods)

Enzo is a cute, round, globular enzyme: a soft periwinkle/lavender blob (like a rounded pillow) with a **square notch on top** (his active site), a small round dimple on his right side (allosteric site), rosy cheeks, two stubby feet. His friend the **substrate** is a small yellow puzzle piece shaped like a "T" whose stem fits his notch.

Make all five on one sheet so he looks identical in each. Cut each to **320 × 320 transparent WebP**.

| File | Mood | Subject |
|---|---|---|
| `assets/mascot/enzo-idle.webp` | idle | Enzo smiling, looking forward, the yellow substrate piece floating just above his notch |
| `assets/mascot/enzo-thinking.webp` | thinking | Enzo looking up and to the side, one tiny hand on his "chin", a small "?" beside him, substrate hovering |
| `assets/mascot/enzo-correct.webp` | correct | The substrate snapped perfectly into his notch, Enzo grinning with closed happy eyes, a little sparkle |
| `assets/mascot/enzo-oops.webp` | oops | The substrate bouncing off his notch at an angle, Enzo with swirly or X eyes, a wobbly mouth and one sweat drop |
| `assets/mascot/enzo-victory.webp` | victory | Enzo jumping with both arms up, substrate locked in, stars and confetti around him |
